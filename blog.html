Lehrmann v Network Ten [2024] FCA 369: A Landmark Ruling on the Defence of Truth and Public Interest Journalism

Justice Lee's Federal Court judgment exposes a jarring truth about Australia's parallel legal systems: a person can walk free from a criminal trial yet be found, on the balance of probabilities, to have committed the very act alleged. This case summary unpacks the Defence of Truth, the Briginshaw principle, and why Network Ten won while still being criticised for the way they reported the story.

Luchetti v R [2025] NSWCCA 222: When a Lighter is Not a Knife

The NSW Court of Criminal Appeal found that applying the R v Henry guideline to an armed robbery committed with a cigarette lighter was a material error. A case note on the limits of guideline judgments and sentencing in the context of serious mental illness.
